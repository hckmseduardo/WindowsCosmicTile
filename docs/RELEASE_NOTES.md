# CosmicTile v0.2.5

CosmicTile retries moves and resizes separately when a window misses its tile and rechecks windows that shift after tiling. In a three-window layout, a tall window can take a full column when the other two fit alongside it.

On v0.2.3 or later, choose **Check for updates** from the tray. On v0.2.2 or earlier, replace your current copy with the matching Windows x64 or ARM64 download.
