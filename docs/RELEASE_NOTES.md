# CosmicTile v0.2.6

CosmicTile can rearrange layouts with three or more windows around a large window, including stacking its neighbors when they need more space.

In-app updates started from v0.2.6 or later resume tiling on the displays that were active before restart. When upgrading from v0.2.5 or earlier, enable tiling again from the tray after the update.

On v0.2.3 or later, choose **Check for updates** from the tray. On v0.2.2 or earlier, replace your current copy with the matching Windows x64 or ARM64 download.
